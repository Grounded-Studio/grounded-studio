Delete your data

Circuit Trace has no accounts. Most game data is stored only on your device. A few features send limited data to services we use; this page explains how to delete each part.

1. Local game data (on your device)

  1. Open your Android device Settings
  2. Tap Apps → find Circuit Trace
  3. Tap Storage & cache → Clear storage (or simply Uninstall the app to remove everything)

This deletes level progress, best scores, Daily and Time Trial history, stars, settings, chosen theme, player name and cached ad consent choices.

2. Friends & Challenges data (Google Firebase)

Only if you used the Friends feature: your anonymous user ID, display name, friend code, push-notification token, friends list, and the challenges and attempts you sent are stored in Google Firebase (EU region).

  • Request deletion with the button below (subject: Delete my Friends data). We remove your user record, friendships, challenges and attempts within 7 days.
  • Clearing app storage alone does not remove this server-side data.

3. Anonymous gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics)

The game sends anonymous statistics (a random ID created on your device, session info, device type, Android and game version, and events like which level you completed) so we can tune puzzle difficulty. No name, email or advertising ID is included.

  • Clearing storage or uninstalling creates a new random ID, so new data can't be linked to your old data.
  • To delete data already sent, request deletion below; we forward it to GameAnalytics.

What is kept

  • Google Play purchase history (Remove Ads, Theme Pack and the visual FX packs) is managed by Google Play, not by us. Restore or refund via play.google.com/store/account
  • Google Play Games achievements and cloud save (only if you signed in) are managed by Google in your Play Games profile.

Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Circuit Trace uses Google AdMob to show ads. Ad-related data (Advertising ID, IP-derived location, general usage signals) is processed by Google, not by Grounded Studio. To request deletion:

  1. Reset your Android Advertising ID: device Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  2. Manage or delete Google ad data at myadcenter.google.com
