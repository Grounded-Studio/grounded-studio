Delete your data

Circuit Trace does not use accounts or servers. All game data (progress, stats, settings) is stored locally on your device. Follow the steps below to delete it.

How to delete your local game data

  1. Open your Android device Settings
  2. Tap Apps → find Circuit Trace
  3. Tap Storage & cache → Clear storage (or simply Uninstall the app to remove everything)

What is deleted

  • Level progress and best-move records
  • Daily Challenge history and Time Trial best scores
  • Sound preference, color theme, player name
  • Cached AdMob settings and consent choices

What is kept

  • Google Play purchase history (Remove Ads €2.99) — managed by Google Play, not by us. Restore or refund via play.google.com/store/account
  • Nothing is retained on our servers — because we don't run any

Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Circuit Trace uses Google AdMob to show ads. Ad-related data (Advertising ID, IP-derived location, general usage signals) is processed by Google, not by Grounded Studio. To request deletion:

  1. Reset your Android Advertising ID: device Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  2. Manage or delete Google ad data at myadcenter.google.com

Prefer email? Send us a request and we'll confirm within 7 days:

Request deletion via email

Retention: because Circuit Trace stores no data on any server operated by Grounded Studio, there is no retention period on our side. Local device data is deleted immediately when you clear storage or uninstall.
