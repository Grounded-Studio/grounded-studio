This website (grounded-studio.app)

  • Analytics and advertising measurement — only with your consent. If you click "Accept" in the cookie banner, we load Google Analytics 4 and the Meta Pixel to count visits and clicks on Google Play links. If you click "Decline", these scripts are not loaded. Your choice is stored in your browser (localStorage).
  • Campaign links — also only with your consent. If you accepted and arrived through a campaign link (for example with utm_source), those parameters are kept for your browser session and passed on to Google Play links, so we can see which campaign led to an install. If you decline, nothing is stored or passed on.
  • Contact form. Your name, email address, topic and message are sent through our own server (hosted by STRATO in Germany) to our inbox. We use them only to reply.
