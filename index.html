About Grounded Studio
Grounded Studio is an independent Android app and game studio based in the Netherlands. We build purposeful, offline-first apps and focused games for daily life — bootstrapped, no VCs, no ads on gameplay, no dark patterns. Founded by a solo developer, the studio ships BuildExact, FreshGarant, and the upcoming puzzle game Circuit Trace.
Yes, the core apps are free to download on Google Play. FreshGarant has an optional PRO upgrade for AI features, family sharing and cloud backup. BuildExact has an optional PRO tier at €5.99 per month unlocking unlimited projects, client management, quote and invoice PDFs, and recurring invoice templates. Circuit Trace will be fully free with no ads on gameplay.

BuildExact
BuildExact is an Android app for Dutch and Belgian tradespeople. It calculates material needs (tiles, laminate, paint, wallpaper, insulation, concrete, gravel), manages clients, generates branded quote PDFs with logo and digital signature, and converts quotes into invoices in one tap. Free to download with an optional PRO tier at €5.99/month for unlimited projects, client management and recurring invoice templates.
BuildExact is built for Dutch and Belgian sole traders and small contractors — tilers, painters, plumbers, gardeners, floor layers and general builders. It covers everything from measuring a room and calculating materials with cutting waste to generating branded quotes, managing clients, and sending invoices via WhatsApp with the 06 number automatically converted to 316 format.
PRO costs €5.99 per month and unlocks unlimited projects, full client management, branded quote and invoice PDFs with your logo and digital signature, a materials price book with separate cost and sale price (margin stays private), one-tap quote-to-invoice conversion, and recurring invoice templates for maintenance contracts (weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly). No contract, cancel anytime.
FreshGarant
FreshGarant is an Android app that helps you track food expiry dates and reduce food waste. Scan barcodes with your camera, snap photos of your fridge for AI-powered scanning (Gemini), get timely notifications before products expire, generate shopping lists automatically, and — with PRO — get AI-generated recipes based on soon-to-expire ingredients plus family sharing for up to 6 members.
Open FreshGarant, tap Scan Fridge, and take a photo of your fridge with the door open. Gemini AI identifies each visible product and adds it to your inventory with an estimated expiry date. You can review and adjust before saving. Works with individually packaged items, produce, and receipts photographed the same way. Everything is processed via a secure API — the photo is not stored beyond the scan.
PRO unlocks AI-generated recipes tailored to your soon-to-expire ingredients (powered by Gemini), family sharing for up to 6 members so everyone sees the same fridge in real time, cloud backup across devices, and hands-free cook mode — a recipe reader that scrolls itself while your hands are covered in flour.
Circuit Trace
Circuit Trace is a minimalist logic puzzle game for Android, coming soon on Google Play. Connect coloured dots by drawing a single path per colour, tile every cell of the grid, and restore the circuit. 100 handcrafted levels ramp from gentle warm-ups to dense grids that test your planning. Fully offline, no ads on gameplay, free.
Circuit Trace is currently in closed beta on Google Play, with a public launch planned soon. The exact date depends on beta feedback — the studio would rather ship it right than rush it. Follow Grounded Studio for announcements, or join the closed beta to get access before public launch.
Send an email to contact@grounded-studio.app with the subject "Circuit Trace beta" and include the Google account email you use on your Android phone (this is what Play Store closed testing requires). You'll be added to the tester list and get an install link within a few days. Playtest feedback is welcome but not required.
