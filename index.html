FreshGarant

by Grounded Studio
● App 01

Never waste
food again

FreshGarant automatically tracks when your products expire. Scan barcodes, receive smart notifications, and save money by preventing food waste.

  • 📱

    Barcode Scanner

    Scan products directly with your camera to automatically look up product data and expiry dates.

  • 🔔

    Smart Notifications

    Receive timely push notifications before products expire, so you can still use them in time.

  • 🛒

    Shopping List

    Automatically generate a shopping list based on what you're almost out of or need to restock.

  • 📊

    Waste Report

    See how much you save and track your progress in reducing food waste at home.

Build with

BuildExact is the smart calculation tool for builders and handymen. Calculate exact material needs, create cost estimates, and keep your projects organized.

  • 📐

    Material Calculations

    Calculate exactly how much material you need for every job — including cut and waste percentages.

  • 💰

    Cost Estimation

    Create detailed cost estimates to manage your budget properly before the job starts.

  • 📋

    Project Management

    Manage multiple projects at once and keep track of progress, costs, and materials.

  • 📤

    PDF Export

    Easily share your calculations and estimates as a clean PDF with clients or colleagues.
