FreshGarant

● App 01

Never waste
food again

FreshGarant tracks your food expiry dates so nothing goes to waste. Scan barcodes, get timely alerts, and see exactly what you're saving. Upgrade to Pro for AI-generated recipes, family sharing, cloud backup, and more.

  • 📱

    Barcode Scanner

    Scan products directly with your camera to automatically look up product data and expiry dates.

  • 🔔

    Smart Notifications

    Receive timely push notifications before products expire, so you can still use them in time.

  • 🛒

    Shopping List

    Automatically generate a shopping list based on what you're almost out of or need to restock.

  • 📊

    Waste Statistics

    See exactly how much money you save and track your progress in reducing food waste at home.

  • ✨

    AI Recipes Pro

    Gemini AI turns your soon-to-expire ingredients into personalized recipes. Less waste, more flavor.

  • 👨‍👩‍👧

    Family Sharing Pro

    Share your inventory with up to 6 family members. Everyone sees the same fridge in real time.
