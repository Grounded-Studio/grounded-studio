● Features

Everything for your pet's
health in one place

Made for owners who want peace of mind: nothing forgotten, everything at hand at the vet.

🔔

Reminders on time

Vaccinations, deworming and flea & tick treatments with repeat schedules. A reminder in advance and on the day itself.

📅

One calm Today screen

Overdue, today and coming up for all your pets together. Tick it off and the next date is planned automatically.

💊

Medication & courses

Dose and times of day, ongoing or as a course with an end date. Tap "Given" or "Later (30 min)" right in the notification.

🩺

Vet visits & costs

Reason, notes and costs, with this year's total per pet. Keep a photo of the prescription or invoice.

⚖️

Weight chart

Weigh regularly and spot changes early. In kilos, or in grams for birds.

🧭

Timeline per pet

Everything that happened, by year: vaccinations with batch numbers, visits, medication and weighings.

🐾

Dogs, cats, rabbits, birds

A profile per pet with photo, breed, birth date, microchip and passport number. Suggestions per species.

📄

PDF for the vet PRO

A clear overview with chip number, vaccinations and batch numbers, medication, weight and recent visits. Share or print.

📷

Scan the pet passport PRO

Photograph the vaccination page and AI fills in the dates and batch numbers. Also for invoices and medicine labels. You always check first.

💾

Backup in one file

All pets, records and photos in one file. Keep it in Google Drive or email it to yourself, restore on a new phone.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Delete everything with one tap in Settings.

🌍

Dutch & English

Fully translated. Called Kwispelboek in Dutch and Wagbook in English.

● Kwispelboek PRO

Free to start,
PRO when you need more

All logs, reminders, the weight chart and backups are free. PRO is available monthly (€2.99) or yearly (€19.99), cancel anytime in Google Play.

  • ✓ Unlimited pets (free: 2)
  • ✓ PDF overview for the vet or pet sitter
  • ✓ Scan a pet passport, invoice or medicine label with AI
  • ✓ Unlimited photos per vet visit (free: 1)
  • ✓ Support an independent Dutch maker
