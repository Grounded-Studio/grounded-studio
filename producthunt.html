A note from the maker
Ruud · indie dev at Grounded Studio

Hey! FreshGarant started because I kept throwing away half my fridge every month. €150+ per year in food, gone. The existing apps all wanted me to manually type every product I bought — I gave up after 2 weeks.

So I built the app I actually wanted: snap the fridge, snap the receipt, done. AI does the boring part. When something is about to expire, AI suggests a recipe using it.

The core app is free forever. PRO (AI Photo Fridge, AI recipes, Cook Mode, family sharing, cloud backup) is a one-time purchase with a 7-day free trial. No ads, no subscriptions, no dark patterns.
