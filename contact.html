Before you write

The answer might already be here.

How do I restore PRO or Remove Ads on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Google account you bought it with, then open the app's settings and tap "Restore purchases". Google Play links the purchase to your account, not your device.

How much does PRO cost?

BuildExact PRO is €7.99 one-time. FreshGarant PRO is €2.99 per month or €19.99 per year. Circuit Trace is free; Remove Ads is a one-time €3.99.

Can I request a feature?

Yes — please do. Choose "Feature request" in the form. Many features in our apps started as an email from a user.

I'm a journalist or creator. Can I get press material?

Absolutely. Pick "Press" in the form and tell us what you're working on — we'll send screenshots, logos and a short studio bio.
