Privacy Policy

Circuit Trace — Minimalist Puzzle Game  ·  Last updated: September 28, 2026  ·  by Grounded Studio

✓ Your game progress stays on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share personal data. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob needs to show ads — and you can remove ads permanently for €2.99.

1. Introduction

Circuit Trace ("the app", "the game") is developed by Grounded Studio. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data. The game itself works offline. The only external service is Google AdMob, which is used to show ads (and is disabled entirely after you purchase Remove Ads).

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following data locally on your device so your progress persists between sessions:

🎮 Progress — completed levels, best moves, tier ratings, unlocked sectors

🏆 Stats — total solves, daily challenge history, Time Trial best scores

⚙️ Settings — sound preference, selected color theme, chosen player name

🛒 Purchase state — whether you have bought Remove Ads (verified via Google Play)

This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app removes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Circuit Trace uses Google AdMob to display three ad formats to free users:

  • Interstitial ads — occasional full-screen ads between levels
  • Rewarded video ads — optional, only shown when you tap a "watch ad for extra hint / reset" button
  • App-open ads — occasional ad when you return to the game

AdMob is a service provided by Google LLC. To serve ads, AdMob may collect data including your device's Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, coarse location (derived from IP), device model and OS version, and general app usage signals. This data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's Advertising Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (EU/EEA/UK), the app shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform, UMP) before requesting any advertising data, in compliance with GDPR and the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. You can withdraw consent at any time from the in-game Settings → Privacy options.

You can also reset or opt out of your Advertising ID from your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Circuit Trace offers one optional in-app purchase: Remove Ads (€2.99). Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play only informs the app whether you own the "remove_ads" entitlement. After purchase, all interstitial and app-open ads are permanently disabled. Rewarded video ads remain available since they are player-initiated.

5. What we (Grounded Studio) don't collect

  • No name or email — the "player name" is a local display label only
  • No account or login system
  • No first-party analytics or crash reporting
  • No access to contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, or precise location
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties beyond AdMob's own ad-serving pipeline

6. Permissions

The app requests network permission (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), which is required by AdMob to fetch ads and by Google Play Billing to verify purchases. No other runtime permissions are requested.

7. Children

Circuit Trace is suitable for all ages, but because it uses Google AdMob it is not classified as a "made for kids" app. Users under 13 (or the applicable age in their jurisdiction) should use the app under parental supervision. If you are a parent and believe your child has interacted with ads inappropriately, contact us.

8. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EU/EEA/UK or California, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data related to you. Since we (Grounded Studio) do not directly collect or hold any personal data, most requests should be directed to Google. To withdraw consent for personalised ads within the app, use Settings → Privacy options.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future update changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect it. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at: rsijbers1982@gmail.com
