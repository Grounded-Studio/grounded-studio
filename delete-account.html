Delete your account

You can request deletion of your FreshGarant account and all associated data at any time.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the FreshGarant app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account
  4. Confirm the deletion

Alternatively, you can send us an email and we will delete your account within 7 days.

Request deletion via email

What data is deleted

  • Your account and login information
  • All products and expiry dates
  • Shopping list and recipes
  • Family account and shared data
  • Statistics and waste history

Active Pro subscriptions are not automatically cancelled. Please cancel your subscription via Google Play before requesting account deletion.
