● How it plays

One rule.
Endless focus.

Draw a single path per color, connecting every matching dot. Cover every cell on the grid. No timers. No noise. Just you and the circuit.

🔗

Connect the pairs

Each colour has two matching dots. Trace a continuous line between them without crossing other paths.

🧩

Progressive mechanics

Stars debut in sector 3, checkpoints in sector 4, diodes and the fill every cell rule from sector 5 onward. New idea per sector — never all at once.

⭐

Three tiers per level

Restored = pairs connected. Optimized = solved at or below par moves. Perfect = every cell filled (early sectors) or mastery challenge cleared.

📅

Daily Challenge

A new puzzle every day, seeded globally so everyone plays the same board. Share your result Wordle-style.

🗓️

Weekly Puzzles

Six fresh puzzles per week, one unlocking each weekday — progressively harder from Monday warm-up to Saturday expert. Solve without UNDO for ★★★.

⚡

Time Trial

60-second sprint. Solve as many puzzles as you can — chase your BEST TODAY and your ALL-TIME record.

🌐

Six languages · offline core

English, Nederlands, Deutsch, Français, Español and Português. Gameplay is fully offline — your progress stays on your device.

● Symbols & mechanics

Learn the language
of the circuit.

Every symbol in Circuit Trace has one clear meaning. New mechanics unlock sector by sector — never all at once.

Endpoints

Two dots of the same colour. Connect them with one continuous path — no crossings.

Path

Drag from one dot to its pair. Release halfway → the path stays. Tap the open end to continue.

Fill every cell

From sector 5 onward, the grid must be fully covered to complete a level. Every cell belongs to a path.

Diode · one-way

A chevron arrow. Paths passing through must step in the arrow's direction. Introduced in sector 5 (OVERLOAD).

Star · collect

Must be visited by a path — any colour will do. Introduced in sector 3 (CIRCUIT).

Portal · teleport across the board

Two cells linked by a wormhole — step in one, tap the twin to continue on the other side. Introduced in sector 6 (WARP).

Checkpoint · must visit

A diamond marker. A path must pass through it — outlined means "not yet", filled means "visited". Introduced in sector 4 (CURRENT).
