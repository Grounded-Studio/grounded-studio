● Symbols & mechanics

Learn the language
of the circuit.

Every symbol in Circuit Trace has one clear meaning. New mechanics unlock sector by sector — never all at once.

Endpoints

Two dots of the same colour. Connect them with one continuous path — no crossings.

Path

Drag from one dot to its pair. Release halfway → the path stays. Tap the open end to continue.

Fill every cell

From sector 5 onward, the grid must be fully covered to complete a level. Every cell belongs to a path.

Diode · one-way

A chevron arrow. Paths passing through must step in the arrow's direction. Introduced in sector 5 (OVERLOAD).

Star · collect

Must be visited by a path — any colour will do. Introduced in sector 3 (CIRCUIT).

Portal · teleport across the board

Two cells linked by a wormhole — step in one, tap the twin to continue on the other side. Introduced in sector 6 (WARP).

Checkpoint · must visit

A diamond marker. A path must pass through it — outlined means "not yet", filled means "visited". Introduced in sector 4 (CURRENT).
