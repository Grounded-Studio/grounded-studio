● How it plays

One rule.
Endless focus.

Draw a single path per color, connecting every matching dot. Cover every cell on the grid. No timers. No noise. Just you and the circuit.

🔗

Connect the dots

Each colour has a start and end point. Trace a continuous line between them without crossing other flows.

🟪

Tile every cell

A level is only restored when the whole grid is filled. Every square counts.

⭐

Perfect solutions

Solve a level in the intended move count to earn a Perfect badge. Track your progress toward all-perfect.

📈

73 levels of mastery

From gentle 4×4 warm-ups to dense grids that test how you plan. Difficulty ramps as you play.

🎯

Level select & profile

Set your name, pick any unlocked level, replay for a better score. Everything stored locally on your device.

🌙

Calm by design

Deep-space background, glowing dots, subtle particles. Built to relax your brain, not overload it.
