We build purposeful Android apps for daily life. Bootstrapped, no VCs, no ads, no dark patterns — just software that respects your time and helps you get things done.

What we stand for

Three principles that guide every decision we make.

🎯

Purpose first

Every feature has a reason. If it doesn't help you cook a meal, calculate a wall or save waste — it doesn't belong in the app.

🛠️

Craft over hype

Details matter: correct calculations, exact notifications, subtle animations. Our apps feel good because we sweat the small stuff.

🌱

Independent by design

Bootstrapped, no VC pressure, no advertising. Free features stay free. PRO exists so we can keep building.
