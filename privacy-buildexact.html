Privacy Policy

BuildExact — Measurement & Calculation Tool  ·  Last updated: May 7, 2026  ·  by Grounded Studio

✓ BuildExact works fully offline. All your data stays on your device. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

1. Introduction

BuildExact is developed by Grounded Studio. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy — and in this case, that's straightforward: we don't collect any.

2. Data storage

All data you enter in BuildExact — projects, measurements, calculations, cost estimates, and photos — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share or export it.

📱 Projects & calculations — stored locally on your device only

📷 Photos — stored locally on your device only

📄 PDF exports — generated locally, shared only when you tap "Share"

3. No data collection

We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, address)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Project data, measurements, or calculations
  • Photos

There are no accounts, no login, and no cloud sync in BuildExact.

4. PDF export & sharing

BuildExact can generate PDF files of your calculations and estimates. These PDFs are created locally on your device. Sharing a PDF (via email, WhatsApp, etc.) is entirely your choice and is handled by your device's standard sharing functions. We do not receive or process any shared content.

5. In-app purchases

BuildExact offers optional Pro features available through Google Play Billing. All payment processing is handled exclusively by Google. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment information.

For more information on how Google handles payment data, see Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Camera & photo library

BuildExact may request access to your camera or photo library to add photos to your projects. This permission is only used when you explicitly choose to add a photo. Photos are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

7. Children

BuildExact is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children — and as stated above, we don't collect data from anyone.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if new features are added that change how data is handled. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at: rsijbers1982@gmail.com

1. Inleiding

BuildExact is ontwikkeld door Grounded Studio. Dit privacybeleid legt uit hoe de app omgaat met je gegevens. We hechten veel waarde aan je privacy — en in dit geval is dat eenvoudig: we verzamelen niets.

Alle gegevens die je invoert in BuildExact — projecten, metingen, berekeningen, kostenramingen en foto's — worden uitsluitend lokaal op je apparaat opgeslagen. Deze gegevens verlaten je apparaat nooit, tenzij jij er expliciet voor kiest om ze te delen of te exporteren.

📱 Projecten & berekeningen — alleen lokaal opgeslagen op je apparaat

📷 Foto's — alleen lokaal opgeslagen op je apparaat

📄 PDF exports — lokaal gegenereerd, gedeeld alleen wanneer jij op "Delen" tikt

3. Geen gegevensverzameling

Wij verzamelen niet:

  • Persoonlijke informatie (naam, e-mail, adres)
  • Locatiegegevens
  • Apparaat-ID's
  • Gebruiksanalyses of crashrapporten
  • Projectgegevens, metingen of berekeningen
