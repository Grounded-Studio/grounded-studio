BuildExact — Measurement & Calculation Tool  ·  Last updated: July 29, 2026  ·  by Grounded Studio

✓ BuildExact works fully offline. Your projects, measurements, calculations, and photos stay on your device. The only data that ever leaves your device is anonymous ad-campaign measurement — and only after you explicitly opt in.

1. Introduction

BuildExact is developed by Grounded Studio. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data. The app itself is fully offline — your projects, calculations, clients and photos never leave your device. Advertising measurement happens only on our website (grounded-studio.app) via the Meta Pixel, and only after you accept cookies.

2. Data storage

All data you enter in BuildExact — projects, measurements, calculations, cost estimates, and photos — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share or export it.

📱 Projects & calculations — stored locally on your device only

📷 Photos — stored locally on your device only

📄 PDF exports — generated locally, shared only when you tap "Share"

3. What we don't collect

Grounded Studio does not collect, receive, or have access to:

  • Personal information (name, email, address)
  • Location data
  • Project data, measurements, or calculations
  • Photos
  • Client or invoice details

There are no accounts, no login, and no cloud sync in BuildExact.

4. Website analytics (Meta Pixel + Google Analytics 4) — opt-in

The BuildExact app does not contain any advertising or analytics SDK. It sends nothing to Meta or any other third party.

Our website (grounded-studio.app) uses the Meta Pixel to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns on Facebook and Instagram. The Pixel only activates after you accept the cookie banner at the bottom of the page.

Data sent to Meta by our website (only if you tap "Accept"):

• Page visits (which pages you view)

• Clicks on the "Download on Google Play" button (as a Lead event)

• Standard browser identifiers Meta already uses across the web (cookies, IP address)

• Campaign parameters from the URL you arrived with (utm_source, fbclid, etc.)

The Pixel does not read anything from inside the BuildExact app on your phone. It only sees what you do on our website.

You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies for grounded-studio.app, or by using your browser's "Do Not Track" / tracking-protection features. Declining the cookie banner permanently disables the Pixel for your visit.

Meta processes this data as an independent controller. For details, see Meta's Privacy Policy.

Our website also uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-DBG67CDKSP) to understand aggregated visitor behavior — which pages are popular, where visitors come from, and which content leads to Play Store clicks. IP addresses are anonymized before storage. GA4 is loaded only after you accept the cookie banner, and can be disabled by clearing your cookies. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Legal basis: your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

4b. Play Store install attribution

When you click a "Download on Google Play" link on our website, we add campaign parameters (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign) to the Play Store link. Google Play uses these to tell us in aggregate which campaigns brought installs. This is done via Google Play's built-in Install Referrer feature — no data is sent to Grounded Studio directly, and the app itself never receives your identity.

5. PDF export & sharing

BuildExact can generate PDF files of your calculations and estimates. These PDFs are created locally on your device. Sharing a PDF (via email, WhatsApp, etc.) is entirely your choice and is handled by your device's standard sharing functions. We do not receive or process any shared content.

6. In-app purchases

BuildExact offers optional Pro features available through Google Play Billing. All payment processing is handled exclusively by Google. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment information.

For more information on how Google handles payment data, see Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Camera & photo library

BuildExact may request access to your camera or photo library to add photos to your projects. This permission is only used when you explicitly choose to add a photo. Photos are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

8. Children

BuildExact is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if new features are added that change how data is handled. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
