How AI meal planning actually works

Modern AI meal planners use LLMs (like Gemini) to solve a multi-variable optimization problem:

  1. Inventory — what's in your fridge/pantry?
  2. Expiry — what needs to be used first?
  3. Preferences — vegetarian? gluten-free? kids-friendly?
  4. Variety — no chicken 4 nights in a row
  5. Time — busy weekdays vs relaxed weekends
  6. Missing ingredients — auto-fill the shopping list

Doing this manually takes 45 minutes. AI does it in 2 seconds — and typically finds better combinations than humans do.

Common questions

Does AI actually understand food combinations?

Yes, remarkably well. Gemini is trained on millions of recipes and understands flavor profiles, cuisine styles, dietary restrictions, and cooking techniques. It won't suggest pineapple with lasagna (unless you're into that).

Can I regenerate if I don't like the menu?

Yes. FreshGarant PRO allows 5 regenerations per day. You can also replace individual dishes without redoing the entire week.

What about picky eaters and allergies?

Set preferences once in Settings: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, no pork, no seafood, etc. AI respects them every time.

Does it work without internet?

The generation itself needs internet (Gemini runs in the cloud). But once generated, the menu is stored locally so you can reference it offline.

Pro tips to get the most out of it
