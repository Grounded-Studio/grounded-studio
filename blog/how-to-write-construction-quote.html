How to Write a Winning Construction Quote (Free Template & Tips)

📅 July 16, 2026 ⏱ 6 min read ✍️ Grounded Studio

Sending a solid quote is half the sale. A vague one-liner in a WhatsApp message often gets ignored — a proper PDF with your logo, clear line items and payment terms gets accepted. In this guide we show you exactly what every construction quote needs, how to price it, and how to generate the PDF in seconds.

What every construction quote must contain

Whether you're a solo contractor or a small builder, every quote needs the same core sections. Missing one? Your client has an excuse to delay or negotiate.

Step 1: Measure and calculate materials first

Before you can quote anything, you need accurate quantities. Guessing material amounts is the #1 reason quotes turn into losses. Measure the surfaces, add cut waste, and convert to real units (m², m³, litres, pieces).

Step 2: Price each line properly

For each line item you need quantity × unit price. Unit prices come from three places:

  1. Material cost per unit — check with your supplier or use a saved materials library so prices auto-fill.
  2. Labor — either hourly (hours × rate) or as a fixed fee per m² if you have your own rules of thumb.
  3. Extras — waste allowance, disposal costs, transport, tool rental. Include them or absorb them, but decide up front.
Line total
Total = Quantity × Unit price
Example: 20 m² tiles × €24.50/m² = €490.00

Step 3: Add labor as its own line

Even if you priced everything per m², add a separate labor line. It's transparent and it makes negotiations easier — if a client pushes back on price, you can adjust hours instead of touching material costs.

Labor line
Labor total = Hours × Hourly rate
Example: 12 hours × €65/hr = €780.00

Step 4: Discount, VAT and grand total

Round out the quote with three totals that most clients look at first:

  • Subtotal — sum of all lines + labor
  • Discount — optional, applied as a percentage on the subtotal
  • VAT — 21% in the Netherlands (9% for certain renovations after 2 years)
  • Grand total — the big bold number, in brand colour
Full formula
Total = (Subtotal − Discount) × (1 + VAT%)
Example: (€1,270 − 5%) × 1.21 = €1,461.24

Step 5: Payment terms & validity

Two dates matter:

  • Valid until — typically 14 or 30 days. After that you can revise the price if material costs jumped.
  • Payment terms — 14, 30 or 60 days after invoice. Print your IBAN clearly so there's no excuse to delay payment.

Step 6: Signature line

Add a signature line at the bottom for both parties — your own signature (auto-added from your business profile) and a blank line for the client to sign and return. A signed quote is legally much stronger than a verbal agreement.

5 quick tips to win more quotes

  1. Send fast. Most clients pick the first solid quote. Aim for same-day PDF delivery.
  2. Break down every line. Clients trust detail. One line saying "renovation" screams overcharge.
  3. Add a personal intro. Two sentences at the top ("Thanks for the visit last Tuesday…") lifts you above the copy-paste crowd.
  4. Follow up after 3 days. Track quote status per client — a friendly nudge often closes the deal.
  5. Always sign. A hand-drawn signature at the bottom builds trust instantly.
