Payment QR Code on Your Invoices: Get Paid Faster with an EPC QR Code

📅 5 November 2026⏱ 5 min read✍️ Grounded Studio

Every invoice that is paid late costs you time: checking your bank, sending a reminder, calling. Part of that delay is simple friction. The client has to open their banking app, type a long IBAN, the amount and the invoice number, and one typo later the payment ends up unmatched. A payment QR code removes that step.

What is an EPC QR code?

The EPC QR code is a European standard from the European Payments Council for SEPA credit transfers. In Germany it is known as GiroCode. The code contains the name of the payee, the IBAN, the amount and the payment reference. The client scans it in their banking app and sees a transfer that is ready to confirm.

Why it helps

  • No typing errors. A wrong digit in the IBAN or the reference is the most common reason a payment cannot be matched to an invoice.
  • Lower threshold. Paying takes ten seconds instead of a few minutes, so it happens straight away instead of "later".
  • Correct reference. The invoice number is in the code, so you can match the payment instantly.

Which banking apps scan it?

Support is widespread in Germany, Austria, Belgium and Finland, and growing elsewhere. Not every bank supports it yet, and some only scan it from a photo or PDF. That is why the IBAN, amount and reference should always stay printed in plain text next to the code, so nobody depends on the scan.

💡 The code is not a payment link

An EPC QR code contains only transfer details. It does not open a website and it cannot take money from anyone. The client still confirms the transfer in their own banking app, which is why clients trust it more than a payment link in a message.
