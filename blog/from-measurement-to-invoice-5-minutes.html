From Measurement to Invoice in 5 Minutes — Complete Workflow for Contractors

📅 August 4, 2026⏱ 7 min read✍️ Grounded Studio

You're at the client's house, measuring a bathroom floor, and you promise: "I'll send a quote tonight." Then you spend 90 minutes wrestling with Excel, typing something quickly in Word, using a calculator to figure out materials — and the next day you can't remember what you actually entered. Sound familiar?

In this walkthrough you'll see the complete workflow from measurement to sent invoice using one concrete example: a 4 m² bathroom floor. All in one app, all digital, all saved per client. Total time: under 5 minutes.

The job: 4 m² bathroom floor

You're at Mrs. Smith's house in Manchester. She wants to replace the bathroom floor — 2.0 × 2.0 m, so 4 m². Grey 30×60 cm tiles, and the job needs to be done next week. You pull out your phone, open BuildExact, and get started.

💡 Why mobile working pays off

Research from UK trade associations shows: contractors who send quotes within 24 hours after the site visit win the job 2× more often. Working on-site eliminates that 24-hour gap entirely.

Step 1: Enter the measurement

Step 1
⏱ 30 seconds

Tap "New project" → "Area"

Enter length (2.0 m) and width (2.0 m). App calculates 4 m² instantly. Save under client name "Mrs. Smith — Manchester".

No more separate notepad. No risk of forgetting measurements. Everything in the app, linked to the right client.

Step 2: Calculate tiles with cutting waste

Step 2
⏱ 40 seconds

Choose "Tiles" calculator

Enter: area 4 m², tile size 30×60 cm, cutting waste 10%. App calculates: 22 tiles + 10% margin = 25 tiles.

Cutting waste is one of the most forgotten factors in DIY calculations. 10% is standard for straight walls; increase to 15% for diagonal patterns or lots of obstacles.

Step 3
⏱ 45 seconds

Step 4: Add labor

Step 4
⏱ 20 seconds

Tap "Add labor"

For a 4 m² bathroom floor, budget ~6 hours (remove old floor, prep, tile, grout). At £55/hour that's £330.

Always put labor as a separate line, not hidden in the m² price. Clients see exactly what they're paying for — and if they push back on price, you can adjust hours without touching material costs.

Step 5: Generate the quote PDF

Step 5
⏱ 30 seconds

Tap "Create quote"

The app bundles all lines into a professional PDF:

  • Your logo and business details at the top
  • Client details for Mrs. Smith
  • Quote number auto-incremented (2026-058)
  • All lines with description, quantity and price
  • Subtotal, VAT, total
  • Your signature (set once, added always)
  • Payment terms and bank details

Step 6: Send and follow up

Step 6
⏱ 30 seconds

Tap "Share" → WhatsApp or email

The PDF goes straight to Mrs. Smith — before you've even left your van. Then set the quote status to "Sent". From your client overview you'll see exactly which quotes are still open.

No reply after 3 days? Send a quick follow-up. Data shows: quotes with 1 follow-up have a 68% acceptance rate, without follow-up just 42%.

Step 7: Convert quote to invoice

Step 7
⏱ 45 seconds

Client says "Go ahead"? Tap "Create invoice"

All quote lines carry over 1-to-1. Just fill in an invoice number (auto-generated), invoice date, and payment term (14 or 30 days). Tap "Generate PDF" and send.

For your admin: all invoices are saved per client with status Draft / Sent / Paid / Overdue. At the end of the quarter, export everything for your accountant in one go.
