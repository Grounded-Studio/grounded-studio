Invite a Friend, Both Get 1 Month Free PRO — Up to 5 Months on the House

📅 July 27, 2026 ⏱ 4 min read ✍️ Grounded Studio

I've never liked how most referral programs work. They either give you one side of the deal (a discount for the referrer, nothing for the friend), or they hand out points nobody knows how to redeem, or they need a credit card upfront. FreshGarant's new referral system is basically the opposite of all that.

Here's the deal in one sentence: you send a 6-letter code, your friend types it in, and both of you get 30 days of PRO — no card, no strings, done.

How it works, step by step

1

Find your code

Open FreshGarant, tap your profile icon, then "Invite friends". Your unique 6-letter code is right there. It never changes and never expires.

2

Share it (one tap)

Hit the WhatsApp button and it opens with a pre-written message. You can also copy the code and paste it anywhere — text, email, Instagram DM, wherever your people are.

3

Friend installs and enters the code

They download FreshGarant, tap "I have a code", and enter your 6 letters. They immediately get 30 days of PRO on their new account.

4

You also get 30 days added

Server-side, we verify the redemption and push 30 days of PRO to your account. If you're already a PRO user, it extends your next renewal by a month. If you're on the free tier, PRO unlocks immediately.

5

Repeat — up to 5 times total

Each successful invite (up to 5) adds another 30 days for you. That's 5 months of PRO for the cost of 5 messages. Your friends get their month regardless of how many people you invited.

What "PRO" actually unlocks

Just so it's clear what you're both getting:

  • AI photo fridge scan — one photo, whole fridge added automatically
  • Receipt scanner — OCR your supermarket receipt into your inventory
  • Barcode price tracking — history and cross-store comparison
  • Cook mode — hands-free recipe reader with per-step timers
  • Family sync — share your fridge inventory with up to 4 housemates
  • Unlimited product history — free tier keeps 60 days, PRO keeps everything

Full PRO retails at €2.99/month or €14.99/year. Five months invited = €14.95 of value in your pocket, plus your friends get a real trial without pulling out a card.

Why we built it this way

Most food apps grow through paid ads. FreshGarant runs a small budget, but I've never been comfortable with the maths — €0.40 per install, half of them uninstall within a week, and it doesn't build any community. Referrals do something different: people who join via a friend's code stick around roughly 3x longer than those who click an ad. Not surprising — a friend recommendation carries actual signal.

So the referral program isn't a marketing gimmick, it's how I'd rather grow this app. Both sides win, no card is required, and the code system is enforced server-side (via Firestore) so nobody can farm codes with fake accounts. Simple, honest, effective.

Practical tip: send it to your housemate first

The single easiest first invite: whoever else lives in your house. They can use PRO's family-sync feature to share your fridge inventory in real time — so when you scan yoghurt on Monday, they see it Monday. That alone is worth the month for many households, and it takes 15 seconds to set up.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my referral code?

Open FreshGarant, tap your profile icon, then "Invite friends". Your unique 6-letter code appears there — share it via WhatsApp, message or email with one tap.

How many friends can I invite?

You can invite an unlimited number of friends, but only the first 5 successful invites reward you with 1 month of PRO each — up to 5 months total. Your friends always get their 1 month regardless.

Do the PRO months stack?

Yes. Each successful invite adds another 30 days. Invite 3 friends this month, you get 3 months of PRO added to your account.

Is this really free? What's the catch?

No catch. Both sides get full PRO features for 30 days at no cost. No card is required to redeem a code. After the free month it reverts to the free tier unless you subscribe.

Can I use a friend's code if I've already been using the app?

Yes — you can redeem one referral code even if you've had the app for a while. Just make sure you haven't previously used a code. If you're already PRO, the 30 days get added to your next renewal.
