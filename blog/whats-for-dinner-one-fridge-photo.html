What's for Dinner? One Fridge Photo, Three Dinner Ideas

📅 5 November 2026 ⏱ 3 min read ✍️ Grounded Studio

Every evening, millions of people open the fridge, stare at it for a minute and close it again. There's food in there. There just isn't a dinner in there — not one you can see, anyway. That moment is where a surprising amount of food waste starts.

So we built a feature for exactly that moment. It's called What's for dinner? and it's the biggest addition to FreshGarant this year.

One photo, three dinners

1

Take a photo of your open fridge

Tap the new card at the top of the home screen and take one photo. No need to tidy up first.

2

Pick what kind of dinner you want

Quick (under 20 minutes), veggie or kid-friendly, for 1, 2 or 4 people. Or skip the photo and tap "From my stock" to use what's already in the app.

3

Get three ideas in seconds

Each dish shows how many of your own products it uses and whether you have everything at home. Products that expire first are used first.

4

Cook, save or share

Open a dish for the ingredients and steps, start the hands-free cook mode, save it to your recipes or share it as a recipe card.

The part we're quietly proud of

While it's coming up with dinner, the AI also recognises the products on your photo. Above the three dishes you'll see something like "5 products recognised" with one button: Add all. One tap and they're in your stock, with storage place and expiry date — so your reminders are up to date without typing anything.

Two jobs, one photo. That was the whole design goal.

Why "expires first" matters

Most recipe apps start from what you feel like eating. That's nice, but it's how the chicken with today's date ends up in the bin while you cook something else. What's for dinner? starts from what has to go first, and builds the dish around it. Every dinner it suggests is a small rescue.

Free, every day

What's for dinner? is free once a day for everyone. With FreshGarant PRO you can ask up to 10 times a day — handy if the first three ideas aren't quite it.
